Sourcing Process

Click a step to explore

Precision Targeting — Eliminating Waste

We operate an extensive internal product database and digital management system. When a client request arrives, we rapidly filter out substandard or low-margin sources — no time wasted on unscreened wholesale platforms. We lock directly onto compliant suppliers with proven mass-production capability and quality assurance.

Leverage Negotiation & Deep Partnership

Bulk purchasing volume grants us deep factory trust. We don't just compare prices — we evaluate product detail differences, delivery reliability and after-sales protection. Low-tier suppliers unable to provide adequate margin or post-sale guarantees are filtered out, ensuring clients receive genuine value.

Rigorous Inspection — We Accept Responsibility

Pre-shipment inspection is non-negotiable. By sourcing through us, quality oversight responsibility is transferred to Spatz Global — eliminating the post-sale risk most feared in cross-border procurement. Clients receive EU-standard goods with full confidence.

Supplier Tiering System

Dynamic factory grading — priority orders flow to Gold tier long-term partners, ensuring supply chain capacity and regulatory compliance from the source.

Gold Tier

Canton Fair core exhibitors. Proven export track record, complete EU certificates, high production capacity, and in-house R&D teams capable of design-around IP protection.

Silver Tier

Manufacturers with solid QMS (ISO 9001), able to accommodate custom packaging and third-party notified body sampling for EU compliance.

Bronze Tier

Standard component suppliers. Require 100% pre-shipment inspection by our QC team before release — ensuring zero-defect delivery.

Profit & Risk Assessment Model: Every new order is evaluated against a strict internal model: Net Profit = Target Price − (Wholesale Cost + Packaging + Logistics + Inspection). Only orders exceeding our safety margin threshold are accepted — protecting clients from margin erosion and suppliers from under-specification risk.
